## 3. THE 1,100x CLONING TRAP (outlier science done right)

- **Outlier definition:** a video performing **10x–100x above its own
  channel's average**; 70% of all YouTube views now come from the
  **Homepage, not Search** — outliers show what the algorithm is
  pushing RIGHT NOW.
- Workflow: find small-channel outliers (big-channel outliers are
  noise — a 50K-view video on a 2K-sub channel is signal; the same
  numbers on a 2M-sub channel are nothing) → extract the pattern →
  build YOUR title/thumbnail/idea around the insight.
- **The trap (measured):** a real cluster of 10 near-identical
  channels — 83–88% semantically identical — had average views from
  **516 to 568,832 (1,100x spread)**. Format cloning is now free
  (AI), so it is worth NOTHING. The spread is the invisible layer:
  persona, execution, taste, trust.
- **Verdict for Monarch:** clone FORMAT DNA (structure laws), never
  clone surface. Our differentiator stack: stickman identity +
  law-gated quality + audit numbers. That's the un-copyable layer.
