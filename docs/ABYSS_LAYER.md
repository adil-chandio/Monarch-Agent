## B. THE PEAK-END DOCTRINE (Kahneman secretly runs the 2026 meta)

The science: people judge an experience by its **PEAK moment + its
END** — not its average, not its duration (**duration neglect**).

- Cold-pressor study (Kahneman/Fredrickson 1993): subjects preferred
  repeating a **60s + 30s-longer** ordeal over a plain 60s one,
  because the longer version ended slightly warmer. More pain, chosen
  freely — because of a better END.
- Colonoscopy replication (N=154): remembered pain = peak + final
  moments. Adding a calm 3-minute tail IMPROVED the memory without
  reducing the procedure.
- The symphony example: a glorious 20-minute symphony "ruined" by a
  screech at the end — the screech didn't touch the experience, it
  destroyed the MEMORY of it.
- Boundary condition: a satisfied END can rewrite a dissatisfying
  middle (waiting-line study).

**Why this is the abyss key for 2026:** the #1 long-form ranking
signal is now **viewer satisfaction** (surveys + returns) — and a
survey is a REMEMBERED judgment. Peak-end is literally the
engineering spec for satisfaction:
- Design exactly **ONE deliberate PEAK** (the wow moment — Beast's
  3-min re-engagement is a peak placement, not a random trick).
- **Engineer the END** (abrupt + satisfying payoff; no fade, no
  "thanks for watching" — every curve-doctrine says it, peak-end
  explains WHY: the end is half the remembered experience).
- Shorts version: the loop ending IS an engineered end — end frame =
  start frame makes the memory of the end hand the viewer back to
  the beginning (rewatch = the same experience re-entering memory).
- Duration neglect validates compression: a denser 22-45s Short beats
  a longer one even at equal content — the memory doesn't reward
  length.

## C. GAZE PHYSICS (eye-tracking: attention obeys the eyes)

Lab-grade eye-tracking findings, robust across contexts/tasks:
- **Direct gaze captures and holds attention**: faces looking AT the
  viewer are viewed longer than averted-gaze faces, regardless of
  background, setting, or task — and are rated more attractive.
- **Gaze cueing**: viewers' attention shifts to whatever the face
  LOOKS AT — spontaneously, faster, and they dwell longer on the
  cued object; gaze is **harder to override than arrows**.
- Implication ladder for Monarch (all compatible with the stickman
  design — dot eyes are compositor math later):
  - HOOK frames: character eyes → AT CAMERA (stop the scroll;
    direct-gaze capture).
  - EXPLANATION frames: character eyes → AT THE OBJECT (the viewer's
    attention is dragged to the cued subject — free attention
    direction, no motion needed).
  - Thumbnail: same two modes = "connection" vs "pointer"; pick per
    beat intent, never random.
- This gives the future overlay/audit waves a MEASURABLE pattern:
  eye-direction must MATCH beat intent (a hook with averted eyes =
  wasted first frame).
