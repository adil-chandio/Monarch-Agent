# MONARCH CAM — STRICT CREATIVE & RIGHTS LAW

Fail-closed. These are not guidelines; a violation blocks the stage.

---

## §LANGUAGE

**All commentary must be English.** No exceptions, no mixed-language VO, no translated
cadence.

## §VOICE

Natural, conversational **US/UK Tier-1 creator speech**.

Never:

- robotic voice
- literal-translation cadence
- forced slang
- exaggerated announcer performance
- fake viral shouting

Target: a knowledgeable person talking to a friend about something genuinely wild — calm
confidence, no performance.

## §VISUAL

- premium **9:16** vertical
- **1080 x 1920**
- **30fps**
- clean dynamic captions
- subtle SFX
- smooth premium transitions
- clear **raw sound** where useful (real reactions beat music)
- strong visual contrast
- large, legible text for TV / mobile / tablet

## §NEVER USE

Visual clutter:

- arrows inside the actual video
- hook-score UI
- numeric rating UI
- ranking-card clutter
- gold arrows / target circles
- big obstructive panels
- tiny captions
- fake "viral" graphics

Structure:

- a forced "Top 10" structure when the story does not need it
- generic countdown filler

Truth and safety:

- **misleading animal-attack claims**
- **graphic animal harm**
- **watermark hiding / removal / cropping**
- **AI-created fake "caught on camera" wildlife visuals**

> Note: `monarch/schemas.py` already bans `"top 10"` and `"you won't believe"` in
> `GENERIC_TITLE_BANS`. The channel law and the base agent law agree — the base gate is the
> floor, this list is the ceiling.

## §IF CLEAN SOURCE MEDIA IS NOT AVAILABLE

**Do not secretly substitute AI visuals or watermarked reposts.**

Continue sourcing better real footage. Report the stage as `BLOCKED` (see `WORLD.md` §4) and
either drop the scene or wait for a licensed source. Substituting is the one failure this
channel cannot recover from, because the entire brand promise is *"this really happened"*.

---

## §RIGHTS, REUSED CONTENT AND SOURCE STANDARD

Monarch Cam must be a **genuinely transformed wildlife storytelling product** — not a clip
uploader.

### For every external clip, all of these

1. identify the **original creator/filmer** or legitimate licensing agency
2. record **source URL, creator, date, location, source type**
3. obtain the **clean master footage**
4. obtain **written permission / licence** for monetised YouTube Shorts **and** long-form use
5. record the **required attribution** exactly as specified
6. save the permission/licence **proof in the footage ledger**

### What is NOT permission

- "No copyright intended"
- generic fair-use wording
- captions
- editing
- voice-over
- credit in the description

Any of those alone = **no rights**. Stage 5 fails closed and the scene cannot be selected.

### What every final video must add (the transformation test)

- original English narration
- a clear editorial question
- a real storyline
- specific factual context
- meaningful scene selection and sequencing
- new visual explanation where needed
- original captioning / editing / sound design
- a unique Monarch Cam point of view

If a video would still make sense with Monarch Cam's narration removed, the transformation
is insufficient — go back to Stage 7.

### Honesty law

Do **not** say or imply that editing another creator's raw video makes Monarch Cam the owner
of the raw footage. Attribution stays visible and accurate.

### Synthetic / altered-content disclosure

Every delivery states explicitly whether a synthetic/altered-content disclosure is required
(deliverable #17 in `delivery.md`). Decide only after reviewing the actual final video against
the platform's current rule. Do not pre-fill `no` from the brief alone. If the finished work
contains only genuine licensed footage, ordinary editing and original non-impersonating
narration, document that factual basis and the applicable current rule; if it contains
realistic synthetic or materially altered scenes/voices, evaluate and disclose as required.
