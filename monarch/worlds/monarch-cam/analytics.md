## DIAGNOSIS

According to the supplied snapshot, the channel has **genuine discovery capacity** — multiple
long-form wildlife breakouts show audiences have clicked and watched Monarch Cam content.

But **loyalty/conversion is low relative to discovery**. ~1.83 subs per 1,000 views means:

- the channel can attract strangers
- it has not converted enough strangers into repeat viewers/subscribers
- historic viral videos still drive too much of the traffic
- it needs a **connected-video system**, not isolated uploads
- **every upload must lead toward one specific relevant next video or playlist**

Do not treat historic views as proof that any generic animal compilation will work. The
biggest wins came from **very specific audience promises**.

## THE TWO PROVEN DEMAND ENGINES

- **ENGINE A** — wildlife too close to people
- **ENGINE B** — visual wildlife discovery / camouflage / hidden creatures

Every new idea must belong to A or B. Anything else needs its own proof before it ships.

## THE SEVEN LAWS THIS DATA PROVES

1. **Specific premise beats generic premise.** Every winner had one readable promise.
2. **"Part N" costs reach.** Never title a sequel as a sequel — give it its own promise.
3. **Browse is the main engine** (68–90% of traffic on every winner) → packaging is the
   primary lever, more than SEO.
4. **End screens are a real traffic source** → every video ships with a deliberate end-screen
   target, never "subscribe" filler.
5. **Age restriction kills distribution and revenue** → no graphic harm, ever.
6. **Completion hovers at 28–38%** → retention is the biggest open opportunity; structure
   must escalate, not list.
7. **Evergreen is achievable** (921 views/48h after 2+ years) → build recommendation
   connections, not one-off spikes.
