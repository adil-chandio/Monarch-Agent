## One-paragraph version of this channel

The supplied snapshot reports real discovery (2.1M all-time views, 3,833 subs) but weak conversion
(~1.83 subs per 1,000 views) and reliance on historic virals. Two reported demand engines exist:
**(A) wildlife too close to people** and **(B) visual discovery / camouflage**. The fix is a
connected-video system where every upload points at exactly one relevant next video or
playlist — not more isolated uploads. Packaging must be specific and honest ("Bears Have
Learned How to Open Our Doors"), never generic ("Bears Caught on Camera… Wait Till the
Last One") and never misleading ("attacks" when it was a sighting).

## What this world is NOT

Not a compilation channel. Not a shock/harm channel. Not a "Top 10 attacks" page. Not a
reposted social-clip account. Not a fake CCTV/AI wildlife channel. Not a loud meme page.
Full list in `identity.md`.
