# MONARCH CAM — IDENTITY

## Channel name

**Monarch Cam** — this is the only name. No aliases, no legacy handles in output.

## Core brand promise

> “Wildlife gets close. Monarch Cam tells viewers what they are really seeing.”

Two jobs in one sentence: **proximity** (it really happened, camera caught it) and
**explanation** (Monarch Cam adds what the viewer could not work out alone).

## Ideal identity

A premium wildlife-entertainment channel built around:

- real camera-caught animal moments
- clear original storytelling
- natural English commentary
- factual context
- tension, humour and surprising wildlife behaviour

## ANTI-IDENTITY — Monarch Cam is never positioned as

- a recycled viral-compilation channel
- a shock/harm channel
- a generic "Top 10 attacks" page
- a copied social-media clip account
- a fake CCTV / AI wildlife channel
- a loud, low-effort meme page

If a packaging idea, thumbnail or script beat only works by pushing the channel into one of
these six, the idea dies. Not "softened" — dropped.

---

## PRIMARY PILLAR — "Too Close: Wildlife in Human Space"

Wildlife entering the space people live in.

- Bears at doors, cabins, cars, porches, windows, campsites, driveways
- Bears learning how to access human food
- Wildlife around homes, roads, vehicles, hikers, security cameras
- Real close encounters explained accurately

The engine is **proximity + explanation**: how close, what the animal is actually doing,
why it is doing it, what happens next.

## SECONDARY PILLAR — "Nature's Hidden Tricks"

Visual discovery. The viewer plays before the video even starts.

- Camouflage
- Animals that are difficult to spot
- "Can you spot the animal before it moves?"
- Animal intelligence
- Weird visual nature moments
- Discovery-based wildlife puzzles

The engine is **curiosity + payoff**: a hidden thing, then the reveal, then a small fact.

---

## PILLAR DISCIPLINE

Both pillars can coexist, but only as **deliberate playlists/series**. Never blended inside
one video without a clear channel connection.

Never mix in, without a channel connection: unrelated cars, disasters, oceans, sharks,
memes, random animals, off-topic wildlife.

Practical test before any idea enters Stage 6:

> Can this idea sit inside "Too Close: Wildlife in Human Space" or "Nature's Hidden Tricks"
> without the viewer feeling the channel changed subject?

If no → not a Monarch Cam video.

## PLAYLIST ARCHITECTURE (the connected-video system)

1. Too Close: Wildlife in Human Space
2. Too Close: Bear Encounters
3. Nature's Hidden Tricks
4. Wildlife Shorts
5. Archive: Caught-on-Camera Classics

Every upload belongs to exactly one playlist and points to exactly one next video or
playlist. See `analytics.md` — this is the direct fix for the channel's conversion problem.

## BRAND SYSTEM

Existing problem: the channel is named **Monarch Cam** but earlier profile branding carried
a **KC / flame** identity. That is a conflict and it causes confusion. Resolve it — one
identity everywhere.

Required unified brand:

- keep the name **Monarch Cam**
- build a clean **"M + camera lens"** mark
- palette: **charcoal / deep navy**, restrained **warm gold**, **white** text
- no flame, no KC, no random identity
- identical across avatar, banner, Shorts, thumbnails, playlists, descriptions, end screens

Banner direction:

```
WILDLIFE CAUGHT TOO CLOSE
Stories • behaviour • unbelievable camera moments
New videos weekly
```
